Why use only representative rows?

The idempotent e fixes representatives 1 and 2 and groups states 1 and 3 in the same fiber. On those two representatives, e and f induce a permutation after compression. Averaging their two permutation matrices gives both column sums equal to one. The constant map sends both representatives to the same fiber, so its weight changes the sums.

There are six rank-two idempotents and one rank-three idempotent on three states. The explorer tests all seven from the fixed marginal probabilities. Rotate the diagram with a horizontal drag or arrow keys when it has focus.
