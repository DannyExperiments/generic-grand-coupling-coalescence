# Q1017 primary-source comparison

Checked 2026-11-27. High confidence in the model and problem match. The bounded search found no exact earlier generic-kernel theorem for all grand couplings. Publication priority remains unestablished.

- Grimmett and Holmes, *Coalescence in Markov Chains*, Journal of Theoretical Probability 39, article 49 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s10959-026-01500-w. Open Problem 3.8, printed and PDF p. 8, asks whether the set of possible coalescence numbers is {1} for almost every kernel under normalized independent Uniform(0,1) entries (Remark 2.4). Author version v2 is dated 17 March 2026 and retains the problem. Theorem 3.5 and Remark 3.7 supply a stationary-mass sufficient condition with positive probability; Lemma 3.13(a) gives existence of a coalescing coupling for the typical kernel. Neither statement quantifies over all couplings at almost every kernel. [Final article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10959-026-01500-w) · [Exact question](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/db99cb9c-74e2-48f8-9312-4ca565a7a774/content#page=8) · [Author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13572)
- The same article's Theorem 4.3 gives a doubly stochastic *lumped* matrix for block couplings. Theorem 4.4 equates these with deterministic coalescence classes; Examples 3.10 and 4.5 exhibit couplings with random classes. Its Theorem 5.3 corrects the block existence criterion from the original *Non-coupling from the past*. The revised earlier chapter (16 October 2025) retains the necessary block conditions and characterizes the maximal-rank permutation case in Theorem 4. Here only one representative row is used per fiber; no lumpability or fixed random partition is needed. [Revised earlier chapter](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~grg1000/papers/cftp-final-vladas-rev25.pdf)
- Högnäs, *Random Semigroup Acts on a Finite Set*, J. Austral. Math. Soc. Ser. A 23 (1977), 481–498, Theorems 1–2 and local-group structure around p. 484, supplies the classical architecture for minimum-rank images and permutations after compression. The proof here rederives the small semigroup fact and projects it onto fixed one-state marginals, where each obstruction has exactly rank minus one independent constraints. The finite family gives the measure-zero conclusion for all couplings simultaneously and an explicit uniform-in-coupling rate away from that family. [Högnäs](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/18EE651A6F9972864DAB71A05F3A1302/S1446788700019637a.pdf/random_semigroup_acts_on_a_finite_set.pdf)
- Checked the exact problem number, author titles, non-block coupling, representative-row/idempotent and affine null-set phrasing. The obstruction family is a sufficient certificate on its complement. The proof gives no converse for kernels on the union. The tail estimate is per fixed certified kernel. Time-dependent correlated map sequences and infinite state spaces are outside this theorem.
