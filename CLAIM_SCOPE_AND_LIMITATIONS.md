# Scope and limitations

The result is for a fixed finite state space and successive independent, identically distributed maps from any grand coupling with the prescribed kernel's one-state marginals. Almost-everywhere statements use Lebesgue measure on the row-sum-one space or an absolutely continuous law there. Normalizing independent uniform entries is the source's particular law.

The affine family contains every obstruction to coalescence, and avoiding it gives a sufficient certificate. The proof gives no converse for kernels on the union. The uniform tail constants depend on the fixed certified kernel. The finite replay gives diagnostics. External peer review, formal verification and an exhaustive priority search remain pending.
